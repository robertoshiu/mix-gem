**Root cause:** `SSRRenderingPipeline` requires PBR materials to write to Babylon's geometry buffer (g-buffer with normals + depth). After v4 replaced everything with custom `ShaderMaterial` (holographic), the g-buffer was empty → SSR shader referenced undefined texture samplers → crash on `undefined['-1']` every frame.

**What was fixed:**
- `ArTrackingScene.tsx`: removed `enableSSR: true` — SSR had zero visual effect on ShaderMaterial scenes, only the crash
- `babylon-pipeline.ts`: added try/catch around `SSRRenderingPipeline` creation as a safety net for any future scenes that attempt SSR on unsupported GPUs
